A different place to explore in every world

Choose a world, then click any screenshot to see it at full size.

Across the app: Home, daily goals, Stats, Habits, Profile, Focus, and the daily map follow your daily-goal world, changing every 15 completed goal days.

Inside the adventure: Each world has 15 playable missions. Finishing them reveals an exit portal, and entering it changes the adventure environment. Adventure progress is separate from daily-goal progress.

The sixth environment is for the bonus story and adventure. The daily journey has five worlds. The 75-day map uses one current-world backdrop across the map.

The forest uses the existing pixel artwork; the new environments currently use simpler illustrated artwork. Character sprites and some decorative props are shared.
